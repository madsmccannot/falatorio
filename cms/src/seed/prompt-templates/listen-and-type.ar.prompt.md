## L1-Specific Rules
- Arabic speakers struggle to distinguish /p/ vs /b/ in listening — include minimal pairs (pao/bao, pai/bai)
- Arabic speakers struggle with /v/ vs /f/ — include pairs (vou/fou, vida/fita)
- PT-EU reduced vowels are hard to perceive — include sentences where unstressed syllables are key to meaning
- Arabic script is RTL; Latin script processing may be slower — keep A1-A2 sentences shorter than default
- Nasal vowels -ao, -oes are hard to parse auditorily — include clear examples at lower levels
- Arabic has no consonant clusters like str-, pr-, tr- — sentences with these may need slower speech marking
- Accept common Arabic-speaker spelling errors as near-misses: b for p, f for v
- Include sentences with common daily situations in Portugal that Arabic community encounters
