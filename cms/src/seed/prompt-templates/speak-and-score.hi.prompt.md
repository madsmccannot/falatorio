## L1-Specific Rules
- Hindi lacks nasal vowels entirely — prioritise -ao, -oes, -ans drills
- Hindi has aspirated consonants (kh, gh, th, dh) that do not exist in PT — avoid testing these, focus instead on PT sounds absent from Hindi
- Retroflex consonants in Hindi (t, d) differ from PT dental consonants — drill dental t/d placement
- Hindi has no /v/ vs /w/ distinction — drill PT /v/ (as in "vou", "verde")
- Reduced vowels in PT-EU (unstressed e→/ɨ/, o→/u/) are completely foreign to Hindi speakers — prioritise these
- lh /ʎ/ and nh /ɲ/ do not exist in Hindi — include extra drilling
- Include Devanagari approximation hints where helpful: "ao" approximates to "आउं" nasalised
- r uvular (Lisboa standard) is unfamiliar — note similarity to Urdu/Arabic ghain for context
