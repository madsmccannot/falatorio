## L1-Specific Rules
- Arabic lacks /p/ — drill p vs b distinction (pao, pai, por, praia)
- Arabic lacks /v/ — drill v vs f distinction (vou, verde, viver)
- Arabic has rich guttural consonants but no nasal vowels — prioritise -ao, -oes nasalisation
- Arabic vowel system (3 short, 3 long) is simpler than PT — drill the 7+ PT vowel distinctions
- Reduced vowels in PT-EU are foreign — unstressed e→/ɨ/ and o→/u/ need extra practice
- Arabic speakers may add short vowels between consonant clusters (estr- → estir-) — include cluster drills
- R uvular in PT-EU is close to Arabic ghain — note this as a positive transfer point
- lh /ʎ/ and nh /ɲ/ do not exist in Arabic — include extra drilling
- Include Arabic script approximation for key sounds where helpful
