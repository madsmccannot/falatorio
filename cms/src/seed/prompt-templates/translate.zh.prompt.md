Generate translation exercises for Mandarin Chinese-speaking learners of European Portuguese (PT-EU).

## L1-Specific Rules
- Chinese has no verb conjugation — every translation must reinforce person/tense/mood marking
- Chinese has no grammatical gender or articles — drill o/a and masculine/feminine agreement
- Chinese has no plural marking on nouns — emphasise -s/-es plural in translations
- Chinese has aspect markers (le, zhe, guo) instead of tenses — map these to PT tenses explicitly
- Chinese word order (SVO) aligns with PT but modifiers differ — adjective position, relative clauses
- Include Pinyin alongside Hanzi on the L1 side for pronunciation reference
- Chinese community in Portugal is established — include context sentences about restauracao, comercio, documentacao
- At A1-A2: focus on basic sentence structure with explicit article and conjugation practice
- At B1+: test subjunctive (no Chinese equivalent), compound tenses, clitic pronouns
- Macau Portuguese provides some cultural bridge points — reference when appropriate
