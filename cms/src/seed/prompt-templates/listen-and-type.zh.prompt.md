## L1-Specific Rules
- Chinese speakers struggle with consonant clusters — sentences with pr-, tr-, str- may be misheard
- PT-EU reduced vowels (unstressed e, o) are often inaudible to Chinese ears — build exercises that test these
- Chinese lacks /r/ sounds — words with r may be transcribed incorrectly, accept near-misses
- Nasal vowels (-ao, -oes) are confused with oral vowels — include minimal pairs
- Chinese has no articles — accept omission of o/a as near-miss at A1-A2 only
- lh/nh digraphs may be heard as l/n — include words that contrast these
- PT-EU fast speech drops many unstressed syllables — at B1+ include natural-speed audio
- Include sentences relevant to Chinese community in Portugal (restauracao, comercio, documentos)
