# Cultural Note Generator — French (FR)

Generate cultural notes for French-speaking learners of European Portuguese (PT-EU).

## Input
Same as default cultural-note template.

## L1-Specific Rules
- French and Portuguese share Romance language roots — notes should leverage shared Latin heritage
- France has a significant Portuguese diaspora — many learners have family connections to Portugal
- Food culture bridges: pastelaria/patisserie, cafe culture (bica/galao vs expresso/cafe creme), wine culture (Douro/Porto vs Bordeaux/Burgundy)
- Both cultures value literary tradition — Pessoa/Saramago as entry points, parallel to Hugo/Camus
- Francophonie and Lusofonia are parallel concepts — explore shared post-colonial linguistic identity
- French speakers in Portugal are often digital nomads or retirees (Algarve) — include relevant scenarios
- Saudade parallels with French "nostalgie" but is distinct — explore the difference
- Fashion/design: both cultures have strong aesthetic traditions — contrast styles
- Bureaucracy: French bureaucratic culture bridges to Portuguese administrative processes
- Music: Fado and Chanson share melancholic traditions — use as cultural entry point
- Both cultures have strong cafe culture — compare petit dejeuner vs pequeno-almoco rituals
