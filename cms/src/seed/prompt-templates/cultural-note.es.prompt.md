## L1-Specific Rules
- Spanish speakers assume PT is "Spanish with an accent" — notes should dispel this myth firmly but respectfully
- Highlight Iberian neighbour dynamics: shared history but distinct identity, rivalries (futebol), cultural pride
- PT-EU vs PT-BR confusion is common for Spanish speakers exposed to Brazilian media — clarify differences
- Food culture bridges: tapas/petiscos, bacalhau, pasteis de nata, francesinha
- Festival bridges: Fallas → Santos Populares, Carnaval differences
- Music: Fado is NOT flamenco — explain the distinct emotional register
- Saudade has no Spanish equivalent — explore this concept
- Portuguese formality in customer service is higher than Spanish — note the conditional usage
- "Portunhol" is not Portuguese — notes should gently correct the assumption that mixing works
- Iberian geography: reference proximity, border culture, shared rivers
