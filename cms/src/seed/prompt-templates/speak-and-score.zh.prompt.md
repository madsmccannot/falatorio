## L1-Specific Rules
- Chinese is tonal; PT-EU uses stress and intonation instead — drill stress patterns, not tones
- Chinese lacks consonant clusters (pr-, tr-, str-, -lh, -nh) — prioritise these heavily
- Chinese lacks /r/ (both tapped and uvular) — drill both r positions
- Chinese lacks nasal vowels — -ao, -oes, -ans are difficult, drill extensively
- Chinese has no grammatical gender — when drilling phrases, include articles to reinforce o/a
- Reduced vowels in PT-EU are foreign — unstressed syllable reduction needs explicit drilling
- Chinese syllable structure (CV) differs from PT (CCV, CVC, CCVC) — drill multi-consonant onsets
- lh /ʎ/ and nh /ɲ/ do not exist in Chinese — drill with Pinyin approximation hints
- Final -s as /ʃ/ in PT-EU (Lisboa) is unfamiliar — include explicit practice
